About

MatData specializes in data consulting services for railway companies, focusing on strategic planning, modeling, and architecture. We also handle development, data engineering, and DevOps challenges. Our expertise is rooted in a deep understanding of the railway system, an extensive industry network, and proficiency in standardization techniques like Linked Data.

We offer courses and workshops to enhance your team's knowledge of railway and data topics. With over 15 years of experience in railway infrastructure data strategy, GIS data science, and the design and development of measurement systems, we have tackled a wide range of data problems.

MatData actively participates in international initiatives such as railML, Openrail, and ERA ontology, ensuring we stay at the forefront of industry standards and innovations.
